# Participation expectations

Help make technical discussion welcoming and evidence-driven. Ask for context,
state assumptions, and critique behavior or code rather than people. Do not use
harassing, discriminatory, threatening, or sexually explicit language. Do not
publish another person's private information.

The repository owner may moderate content or participation to keep the project
safe and productive. Send sensitive conduct reports privately. Good-faith
reports will be handled discreetly and without retaliation.
